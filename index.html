About Me

Who is Pratham Soni?

I think in tables and charts, but I write the slide first. The work that keeps me up at night sits at the seam: finding the one number that changes how a team decides.

Three years moving between SQL queries, Power BI dashboards and Python notebooks - usually somewhere between an analyst's question and a stakeholder's deadline. Outside of work I sketch, follow Formula 1 with embarrassing intensity, and play five-a-side most Sundays. I also took a year to wait tables in Sydney and volunteer for the city's NYE - both excellent crash courses in talking to actual humans.
