Adaptive Learning &
Curriculum Recommender.

A learning platform where students follow a curriculum that re-shapes itself around their last quiz - and teachers see the whole class as one chart.

Context
Final-year project
Year

Stack
Recommender · Web
Award

01The problem

Most online learning is one-size-fits-all: every student gets the same module sequence regardless of where they actually struggle. Teachers, meanwhile, have no compact view of who's stuck on what. The platform tries to close both gaps - adaptive for the student, observable for the teacher.

03Approach

  • Topic-selection flow that scores prior knowledge with a pre-quiz
  • Recommender ranks modules by predicted gain × difficulty fit
  • Post-quiz updates the model so the next session adapts in-flight
  • Teacher view aggregates student progress into a single per-class heatmap with material-allocation controls

05What it taught me

"Personalisation" is mostly a UI promise - the model just ranks. Where the experience either earns trust or loses it is the explanation on the way in (why this topic next?) and the aggregation on the way out (how is the class doing as a whole?).
